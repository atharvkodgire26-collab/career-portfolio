AI EMAIL MARKETING SYSTEM - DAY 22
====================================
SYSTEM: 3-email welcome sequence + promo + follow-up. Reusable across industries.

PROMPT TEMPLATE:
You are an email marketing specialist. Write a [sequence type] for [industry/brand]. Each email needs: 5 subject line options, a body under 120 words, and 1 clear CTA. Tone: [tone].

SEQUENCE TYPES:
- Welcome (3 emails): Welcome -> Value -> Conversion
- Promo: announce a new feature or offer
- Follow-up: reminder with social proof

TESTED ON 3 INDUSTRIES (Sep 2, 2026):
1. SaaS (AutoBook - AI appointment booking for salons/clinics)
   - Email 1: Welcome, introduce product, no pressure
   - Email 2: Show 5 hours/week time savings
   - Email 3: 14-day free trial CTA
   - Tone: friendly, professional

2. Coaching (BrightPath Academy - 10th-12th tutoring, India)
   - Email 1: Welcome, parent-friendly, warm
   - Email 2: Focused preparation > more hours, structured approach
   - Email 3: Free diagnostic test CTA
   - Tone: warm, encouraging, parent-focused

3. E-commerce (TrendKart - Indian fashion, Gen-Z audience)
   - Email 1: Welcome, energetic, casual
   - Email 2: Bestsellers + style tips
   - Email 3: Complete your look, no discount needed
   - Tone: energetic, casual, Gen-Z

4. SaaS Promo (LeadSnap - AI voice lead qualification)
   - Promo: announce AI Voice Calls feature
   - Follow-up: reminder + social proof ("500+ businesses")
   - Tone: confident, clear

KEY LESSONS:
- Tone matters more than content (same structure, different industry = different tone)
- Under 120 words forces clarity
- 5 subject lines per email = A/B testing ready
- Social proof (numbers, testimonials) makes follow-ups credible
